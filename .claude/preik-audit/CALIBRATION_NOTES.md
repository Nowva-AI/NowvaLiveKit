## WS-B follow-up — `keep_world_frame` DONE (37 tests in test_person_calibration.py; 105 with triangulator + tpose)
`refine(calibration, views, bar_ends=None, keep_world_frame=False)` and the same keyword on `calibrate(..., initial=...)` (ignored when initial is None). Rigid alignment (no scale change) of the solved rig onto the initial cameras: each camera contributes its centre + three axis tips at its median depth to the joints; leave-one-out consensus picks the agreeing subset (left-out camera rejoins within 3× subset RMS or 2 mm); 2 cameras → reference camera's pose kept; logs which camera moved; `world_anchor` copied from the initial; `standing_frames = 0`. Private undistort replaced by WS-A's `undistort_keypoints`.
- Noiseless: untouched cameras < 1e-6 mm, moved camera < 1e-6 mm of truth (non-reference AND reference camera moved). Plain all-camera fit would drag untouched cameras 17–25 mm.
- Realistic noise, one camera moved 2° + 5 cm: untouched cameras 9.9 mm / 0.22° from initial (worst 16 mm); moved camera 21.8 mm / 0.40° from truth; absolute joint error 111 → 9.0 mm; 1.6–2.0 s.
- Caveats: a drift refine shifts the world ~1 cm (keep foot anchors/floor; not identical poses); a scale disagreement between initial and BA (height-scaled initial refined with the bar) cannot be absorbed by the rigid fit (centres mismatch up to a few cm); only ONE moved camera is handled — two of three moving is undetected.
